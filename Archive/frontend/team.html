The Core Vision

CallSphere began with a simple question: "Why do we need a SIM card to have an identity?"

We envisioned a world where your email is your passport and your UID is your universal address. By stripping away the dependency on telecom carriers and centralized social media giants, we created a peer-to-peer ecosystem where the user owns the connection.
